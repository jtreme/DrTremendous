The Sound of a Market

Drag the white price line on the graph. Watch the space between Qs and Qd: D's show excess demand (a shortage), S's show excess supply (a surplus). You'll hear it too — shortage climbs, anxious and rising; surplus sinks, heavy and sagging. Find the equilibrium and the market resolves into a calm chord.

Headphones recommended.

Your Task

For each claim, classify every stakeholder as benefiting, bearing pressure, facing mixed incentives, needing more evidence, or missing from the claim. Then use the reveal to explain what would have to be true for your answer to hold.

Remember the labor market

In the farm labor market, the wage is the price. Workers supply labor. Farms demand labor. A minimum wage is a price floor in the labor market; then you trace possible spillovers into food markets.

Reasoning checklist

Use this as the exit-ticket layer after the board lights up.

· Names the labor-market flip: workers supply, farms demand.
· Identifies the direct wage benefit to workers.
· Connects labor costs to the food market.
· Explains that price pass-through is conditional.
· Names conditions such as elasticity, market power, contracts, or mechanization.

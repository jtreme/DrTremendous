Read the fine print

Rankings without
the asterisk.

01

Hidden isn’t zero.

USDA marks protected figures “not disclosed.” They are excluded from rank math—never counted as zero.

02

Real numbers only.

Every displayed national rank comes directly from published USDA data. Commodities North Carolina is not nationally ranked in are labeled “NR” and never assigned a rank. In the draft game only, NR picks take a 50-point penalty.

03

Year + basis, labeled.

Production and sales-value figures come from different years and reporting bases, so sorting by sales value is a broad comparison, not a matched annual series. Each commodity’s basis and year stay attached to it.
